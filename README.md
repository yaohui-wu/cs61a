# CS 61A: Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs

This repository contains my solutions to the labs, homework, and projects for UC
Berkeley's _CS 61A: Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs_ course.
All solutions passed 100% of the test cases and received full credit on the
autograder.

## Course Details

- **Institution:** University of California, Berkeley
- **Course Code:** CS 61A
- **Course Name:** Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs
- **Course Version:** Spring 2024
- **Course Website:** [CS 61A: Structure and Interpretation of Computer
  Programs][course-link]
- **Technologies:** Python, Scheme, SQL
- **Coursework Timeline:** February 18, 2024 – May 17, 2024
